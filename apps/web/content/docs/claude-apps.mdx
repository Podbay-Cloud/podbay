---
title: Claude on desktop and mobile
description: Use the official Claude apps to direct work happening inside your pod.
---

The official Claude apps are the normal way to work with a Podbay pod. Podbay runs Claude Code in
the cloud and connects that session through Claude Remote Control after you sign in.

## What this gives you

- Start a task from Claude on desktop.
- Close the laptop while the pod keeps its workspace and services.
- Check in or continue the same pod session from Claude on mobile.
- Return later to the existing project, tools, and working state instead of preparing your machine
  again.

Your phone or laptop is the interface; it is not where the project runs. Builds, databases, services,
and scheduled work use the pod's resources.

## Open the pod's session

From the Podbay dashboard, open the pod and choose **Continue in Claude** or **Open in Claude**. If
Claude has not been connected on that pod yet, Podbay guides you through the one-time sign-in first.

Suspending a pod stops the active session and its background work. The workspace and Claude login
remain stored; resume the pod before opening it from Claude again.

## What happens while you are away

A running pod can keep declared application services available and perform scheduled agent work even
when the Claude app is closed. An ordinary interactive request does not automatically become a
background job, so tell Claude when something should continue or run again later.

## When to open Podbay

Use the dashboard for machine-level decisions: adding secrets, changing preview visibility,
inspecting health and resources, updating, suspending, or deleting a pod. The browser terminal is
available when direct shell access is useful for diagnosis or recovery.
