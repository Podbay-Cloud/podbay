# skills-registry Specification

## Purpose
Make the skills that ship to pods legible and vettable. Approval lives in a hand-maintained audit
ledger (`skills/registry.yaml`); what actually ships lives in committed `SKILL.md` + `SOURCE.md` files
under `environments/**/.claude/skills/`. Neither is complete alone — the ledger can go stale and a
skill can ship without ever being approved. The registry JOINS them so an admin can see, in one place,
every shipped skill's version, provenance, license, tier, environments, and approval state, plus the
drift between the two. Authoring and vetting stay in git (edit files, regenerate, PR); the derived
index and its admin view are read-only.

## Requirements

### Requirement: Approval is recorded in a hand-maintained audit ledger
The audit ledger (`skills/registry.yaml`) SHALL remain the source of truth for POLICY — each skill's
trust tier, audit state (`passed`/`not-selected`/`superseded`) with who approved it, when, and why,
and any guardrails it is bound by. The ledger SHALL be edited by hand through pull requests, and
generated tooling SHALL NEVER overwrite it.

#### Scenario: Generation preserves the ledger
- **WHEN** the derived skills index is regenerated
- **THEN** `skills/registry.yaml` SHALL be read and left unmodified

### Requirement: The shipped-skill index is generated by joining the ledger with the files
The system SHALL generate the admin index by scanning every `environments/**/.claude/skills/<id>/`
directory — reading each skill's `SKILL.md` frontmatter (id, version, description) and its `SOURCE.md`
provenance (source repo@path, upstream pin, license, and the vendored or authored date) — and joining
each result with the ledger entry of the same id, taking structural facts from the files and policy
(tier, audit, guardrails) from the ledger. Each entry SHALL record which environments use the skill
(`usedBy`, aggregated across every location the id appears). The upstream pin SHALL be either the
vendoring commit SHA or, when that was not recorded, the transport lockfile's content hash — either
identifies upstream drift. The generated index SHALL be a typed artifact the bundled web app can
import, since the deployed app cannot read repository files at runtime.

#### Scenario: A shipped skill with a ledger entry
- **WHEN** a skill ships and the ledger has a matching entry
- **THEN** its index entry SHALL carry version/description/provenance from the files and tier/audit/
  guardrails from the ledger

#### Scenario: A vendored skill pinned by content hash
- **WHEN** a vendored skill's SOURCE.md records a content hash instead of a commit SHA
- **THEN** its entry SHALL carry that hash as its pin, and the admin view SHALL show it is a hash

#### Scenario: A skill used by more than one environment
- **WHEN** the same skill id appears under more than one environment's skills directory
- **THEN** it SHALL produce a single entry whose `usedBy` lists every environment that includes it

### Requirement: Drift between the ledger and what ships is surfaced
The system SHALL flag both directions of drift: a skill that SHIPS but has no `passed` audit in the
ledger SHALL be marked unvetted, and a ledger entry that claims a skills location but has no shipping
files SHALL be marked a stale entry. Ledger entries recording a surveyed-but-rejected or superseded
skill (no shipping location) SHALL NOT be treated as drift, and neither SHALL entries that describe a
non-skill artifact such as a rule.

#### Scenario: A skill ships without approval
- **WHEN** a skill's files ship but the ledger has no entry for it, or its audit is not `passed`
- **THEN** it SHALL be reported as unvetted and SHALL appear in the vetting queue

#### Scenario: A surveyed-but-rejected skill is not drift
- **WHEN** a ledger entry records a skill that was never selected or has been superseded, with no
  shipping location
- **THEN** it SHALL NOT be counted as a stale entry

### Requirement: Admins can browse and triage the registry
The system SHALL expose an admin-only view listing shipped skills with their id, version, tier,
approval state (including who vetted it and when, and the audit rationale when recorded), description,
source with its pin, license, guardrails, and the environments that use them. The view SHALL support
text search across id/description/source/environment/audit notes, and SHALL offer filters for the
vetting queue and for ledger-only entries, ordering unvetted skills first. The view SHALL NOT mutate
skills or approvals — those happen in git.

#### Scenario: The view is admin-gated
- **WHEN** a non-admin requests the skills view
- **THEN** access SHALL be denied (redirected away)

#### Scenario: Vetting queue surfaces unvetted skills
- **WHEN** an admin filters to the vetting queue
- **THEN** only shipped skills without a `passed` audit SHALL be shown

#### Scenario: Search narrows the list
- **WHEN** an admin searches for a term
- **THEN** only skills matching the term in their id, description, source, environment, or audit notes
  SHALL be shown

### Requirement: The generated index stays in sync with its sources
The generated index SHALL be kept current with the ledger and the skill files. When any of them
changes, a check SHALL be able to verify the committed index matches what the generator would produce,
so a stale index is caught rather than shipped.

#### Scenario: Stale index is detected
- **WHEN** a skill's SOURCE.md or the ledger changes but the index is not regenerated
- **THEN** the freshness check SHALL fail, reporting that the index is out of date
