# Licensing — podbay in plain English

podbay is licensed under the **Business Source License 1.1 (BSL)**. The full legal text is in
[`LICENSE`](LICENSE); this file explains what it means for you. If they ever disagree, `LICENSE` wins.

## What you CAN do (no permission, no payment)

- **Self-host podbay** for yourself, your team, or your company's internal use — run as many pods,
  agents, and workspaces as you like.
- **Read, modify, and fork** the source; build your own images; run it air-gapped.
- **Redistribute** your modifications (they carry the same license).
- **Contribute** back (see [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md)).

You do **not** need a commercial license to run podbay for your own or your organization's use.

## The one thing you CAN'T do

- **Offer podbay to third parties as a competing hosted/managed service.** You can't take this code and
  sell "podbay-as-a-service" to other people. That's the managed product; running it for your own use
  is not.

If you want to offer a hosted service, contact the licensor about a commercial license.

## It becomes fully open-source over time

Each released version's code automatically converts to the **Apache License 2.0** three years after that
version is first published (the "Change Date"). BSL is *source-available now, open-source on a delay* — it
protects the young product without locking the code away forever.

## Trademarks are separate

The BSL covers the **code**. It does **not** grant rights to the **"podbay" name or logo** — those are
protected separately. You can run and modify the software; you can't call your fork "podbay" or use the
mark in a way that implies endorsement.

- Everything else: open a discussion on the public repo.
